# Habit growth icon set — generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.

Create one cohesive horizontal set of exactly five equal square mobile-app icons showing five sequential stages of the same plant growing in the same flat frontal soil cross-section: seed, sprout, strong shoot, established habit/young tree, and second nature/mature tree. No text or separators.

Every stage must show the above-ground plant, a horizontal ground line, a wide flat front-facing soil cross-section, and the underground seed or roots. Soil occupies about 35–40% of the icon height; the above-ground portion occupies about 60–65%. Keep the soil shape, height, width, position, color, camera angle, lighting direction, outline weight, safe padding, plant species, leaf shape, and root stylization consistent across all five stages. The layer of soil must be broad, flat, frontal, and horizontal: no visible top plane, side faces, wedge, mound, island, cube, cylinder, pedestal, isometric perspective, or cake-slice geometry. Roots remain fully inside the soil and fully visible. Nothing may be cropped.

1. Seed: seed fully buried near the center of the soil, surrounded by earth particles, with a delicately textured coat, a thin first root downward, and a tiny light-green shoot passing through the soil and barely emerging above the surface with closed cotyledons. Very thin entirely green stem. Subtle warm glow around the seed.
2. Sprout: cracked seed coat remains visible underground; young main root with a few simple side branches; fragile green sprout above ground with two small open leaves, fine veins, gentle highlights, a visible growth tip, and a tiny new leaf pair. Stem is slightly taller and thicker but flexible and entirely green.
3. Strong shoot: developed simplified root system with one strong central root and several neat side roots; sturdy young plant with a thicker stem, several tiers of upward leaves, and small side shoots. The lower stem gradually becomes warm light brown with subtle young-bark texture while the upper stem and twigs remain green. No hard color boundary.
4. Established habit: strong, balanced roots evenly distributed in soil; a formed young tree with a warm natural-brown trunk and main branches, delicate stylized young bark, fresh green tips, and a compact layered crown. No flowers or fruit. Add restrained warm leaf highlights, a few tiny golden particles, and a faint glow around the upper crown only.
5. Second nature: an unmistakably strong mature tree, not a bush, flower, bonsai, or houseplant. Large evenly developed root system with thick main roots and simpler thinner laterals filling the soil. Substantially thicker deep natural-brown trunk with soft mature-bark texture, natural branching, several strong main branches, and a healthy, balanced, layered crown with subtle hue variations and natural gaps. A few green branch tips may remain. Add a calm soft golden-green halo, delicate rays, and a small amount of glowing particles without obscuring the tree, soil, or roots.

Style: premium highly detailed modern vector illustration with gentle 2.5D depth for a polished mobile app; soft rounded forms, clean medium-weight outlines, smooth gradients, delicate inner shadows, soft local lighting from upper left, small highlights, subtle plant/soil/root/wood textures, and layered overlaps. Coherent large silhouettes must remain legible at 64x64 and detailed at 256x256. Roots are contrasted, simplified, medium-weight, and never hairlike or anatomically excessive.

Palette: primary green #47B96B; light green #8EDB74; deep shadow green #1F6F45; warm neutral soil #7B5133 to #A56F45; young wood #B87845; mature trunk #704226; warm neutral highlights #F5E9D2; gold #E7B85C only on stages 4–5.

Output: one wide 5:1 sprite-sheet composition, exactly five stages in equal square cells with equal safe padding and transparent negative space. No visible cells, cards, borders, labels, numerals, captions, UI, frames, or watermark.

Avoid: photorealism, botanical photography, emoji, childish cartoon, plastic 3D render, noisy texture, excessive detail, thin outlines, sharp edges, neon colors, dark or complex scenery, inconsistent plants/perspective/lighting/scale/soil, seed on top of or outside soil, flower pot, transparent soil, missing soil cross-section, hidden or cropped roots, roots outside soil, cropped leaves/crown/trunk, decorative effects covering roots, brown stems in stages 1–2, abrupt wood transition, dead gray wood, dry plant, photorealistic bark, green mature trunk, flowers, bouquet, flowering bush, bonsai, houseplant, isometric or volumetric soil, text, numbers, labels, and watermark.
