Mode: built-in `image_gen`.

Every stage must show the above-ground plant, soil surface, full soil cross-section, and underground seed or roots. Lock the soil shape, plant species, leaf shape, lighting from upper left, visual center, margins, outline weight, and root style. Underground portion is 35–40%; above-ground portion is 60–65%. Roots are bold and legible at 64×64. Stems remain fully green in stages 1–2, gain only a warm tint at the base in stage 3, gradually lignify in stage 4, become a young wooden trunk in stage 5, and a mature trunk in stage 6. Add restrained gold accents only to stages 5–6. No text, labels, flowers, prominent fruit, pots, people, animals, logos, watermark, photorealism, hard 3D, or visual clutter.

Preserve the same plant species, leaf shape, soil-block silhouette, three-quarter camera angle, upper-left lighting, medium outlines, premium vector-like soft 2.5D rendering, palette, root styling, textures, and warm neutral background. Fit the complete subject inside the square with generous padding; never crop the plant, roots, soil, glow, or particles. Show the above-ground plant, clear soil surface, complete soil cutaway, and underground seed or roots simultaneously. Keep roots bold and readable at 64×64. Include no neighboring panels, text, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, frames, pots, flowers, prominent fruit, animals, people, or unrelated objects.
