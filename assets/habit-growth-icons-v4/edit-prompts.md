Use case: precise-object-edit. Change only the small non-root, non-seed objects embedded in the soil. Reduce them substantially and redesign the few remaining objects so they read unmistakably as natural stones. Keep about 3–5 visible stones per soil cross-section, widely and irregularly spaced. Use small and medium irregular asymmetrical pebble shapes with subtly flattened or angular contours, muted desaturated warm gray-brown/taupe colors, one soft highlight, and one quiet shadow edge. The stones must feel embedded in the soil and remain subordinate to the seed and roots. Avoid perfect circles, glossy spheres, nuts, seeds, bubbles, potatoes, and decorative gems.

Preserve the five plants, leaves, stems, trunks, branches, crowns, seeds, root systems, grass lines, soil cross-section boundaries, five-stage progression, positions, scale, proportions, palette, lighting, texture style, transparent background, 5:1 canvas, equal square cells, safe padding, and golden effects. Do not alter the seeds in stages 1–2 or remove, recolor, simplify, cover, or move roots. Keep the soil color and overall texture, seamlessly filling only the places where excess round objects are removed. No text, labels, borders, cards, UI, or watermark.
